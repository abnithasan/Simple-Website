About the Department

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering focuses on providing quality education in computing and emerging technologies.

Our department encourages students to develop technical skills, creativity, and problem-solving abilities.

Academic Programmes

The department offers the following subjects:

  1. Data Structures and Algorithms
  2. Database Management Systems
  3. Computer Networks
  4. Operating Systems
  5. Artificial Intelligence
  6. Software Engineering
